# Kenopsia Reference Style Lock

## Source profile

The supplied `Kenopsia` reference is approximately 79.6 seconds, 16:9, and dialogue-free. Use it as the default moving-image style benchmark for this Skill. Match the profile below at the level of visual grammar, rhythm, texture, and sound, while creating new characters, places, actions, objects, music, and surreal imagery.

## Mandatory style profile

- **Narrative:** non-linear memory montage that alternates an empty or transformed present with incomplete fragments of lived experience.
- **Rhythm:** mostly 2–3 second fragments, interrupted by 5–10 second spatial anchors; the final image is a static 6–10 second wide or medium shot.
- **Framing:** centered iconic compositions, deep corridor or doorway frames, silhouetted figures, tactile inserts of hands, eyes, cloth, paper, dust, glass, or water.
- **Camera:** locked shots for charged objects and empty spaces; gentle handheld tracking for running or passing memories; very slow push or pull only when it deepens absence.
- **Light:** intense backlight, visible sunbeams, controlled overexposure, lens flare, warm golden highlights, and cool cyan-green or gray-blue shadows.
- **Texture:** live-action realism with visible 35 mm grain, soft halation, slight edge softness, restrained chromatic aberration, and no clean digital-advertising finish.
- **Focus:** shallow depth for tactile memory fragments; deeper focus for architectural anchors and the last static shot.
- **Editing:** rhythmic hard cuts aligned to musical beats or turns, with rare light-leak or overexposure transitions and a final fade to black.
- **Motion:** natural movement with restrained slow-motion feeling; allow wind, curtains, dust, water ripples, paper, hair, or shifting light to animate still frames.
- **Sound:** no dialogue by default; original slow lo-fi beat, nostalgic analog synth pad, environmental wind, distant steps, room tone, and small material sounds.
- **Typography:** minimal title or credit cards only when requested; use generous black space and simple sans-serif type without copying names, logos, or exact layouts from the reference.

The video itself is not included in the Skill repository because Lumina's importer does not accept `.mp4`. Keep it as a runtime reference on the canvas.

## Transferable structure

- 00:00–00:14: establish absence through an unfamiliar condition, strong central image, or empty architecture.
- 00:15–00:51: alternate brief memories through matched movement, place, light, or sound rather than linear exposition.
- 00:51–01:03: compress emotion through tactile close-ups, eyelines, floating material, flare, or exposure change.
- 01:03–01:15: return to a longer, still spatial image and let subtle in-frame movement carry the afterimage.
- After 01:15: fade to black; use minimal end text only when requested.

## Translation into family drama

Keep the shape “empty present → memory fragments → altered present.” A simple act of return, clearing, waiting, searching, or leaving can provide the story spine. Each fragment must reveal a changed relationship to the place rather than merely decorate the montage.

Apply these constraints:

- Choose one recurring object or sound rather than stacking symbols.
- Create one new paradoxical image tied directly to absence or memory; it must not reuse the reference's object combinations.
- Keep fragments around 2–3 seconds and present-day anchors around 5–10 seconds.
- Include at least one section with ambience only and no music.
- End with a locked wide or medium shot in which subtle environmental movement carries time.

## Prompt style kernel

Append this concept, adapted to the shot, to every storyboard and video prompt:

```text
Dreamlike live-action memory film, 16:9 cinematic frame, intense backlight and visible sunbeams, warm golden highlights against cool cyan-green shadows, soft halation, pronounced but organic 35 mm grain, slight chromatic aberration, tactile lived-in detail, melancholy without melodrama. Alternate centered or deep architectural compositions with shallow-focus sensory inserts. Use restrained slow-motion feeling, gentle handheld tracking only for moving memories, otherwise a locked camera with internal environmental motion. No clean commercial gloss, dialogue, logos, captions, watermarks, or copied signature imagery.
```

## Do not copy

Do not reuse the combinations of a burning piano, a butterfly on a fingertip, fire inside a flooded room, or the reference's exact corridor-running sequence. Do not reuse its title, faces, wardrobe, shot order, credits, logos, or music. Match the method and sensory grammar—not the protected expression.
